# How to cook Quinoa
#c-note c260817-1235

Prep:5 mins
Total:25 mins
Yield: Makes about 3 cups

## Ingredients
- 1 cup quinoa, rinsed and drained several times
- Pinch of kosher salt

## Directions

1.	This step is optional, but it really brings out the flavor in the quinoa. Before adding the water and salt, you simply place the quinoa in the saucepan over medium-high heat and give it a shake and a stir every few seconds, for a total of about 4 minutes — until it is golden, or until it starts to pop and smell nutty — watch carefully that it doesn’t burn. You can also heat up a couple teaspoons of olive, vegetable or grapeseed oil (per cup of quinoa) before adding the quinoa for even more flavor.
2.	Combine quinoa (1: 1&1/2) or (Basic Ratio1:2) and water, and salt in a saucepan. Bring to a simmer (low boil), then lower heat and cover and cook over low heat until tender, about 15 minutes. Remove from heat; let stand, covered, 10 minutes. The liquid should all be absorbed, and the quinoa will be tender and have sprung a little tendril-y shoot — which is actually the germ of the kernel — indicating it’s done.
3.	When the quinoa is cooked, give it a stir and a fluff with a fork to loosen it up, then cover the pot and let it sit for another 2 minutes or so. You can serve it hot, or you can spread it out in a rimmed baking sheet and let it cool for use in salads and other room temperature dishes.
